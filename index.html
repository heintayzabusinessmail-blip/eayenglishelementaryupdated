Reading Passage: Su Su's Email

Dear Mya, How are you? I hope your family in Taunggyi is well. I am writing to you from my new flat in Sanchaung township, Yangon. I really love my new home! There is a large living room with big windows, and there are two quiet bedrooms. It's got modern wooden furniture and a small, clean kitchen. My neighbourhood is great because there are three lively teashops on my street and a fresh fruit market around the corner. The only problem is the street noise in the morning. The buses and motorbikes are very loud at seven o'clock! My daily routine is quite busy now. I get up at half past six every morning and walk to the local market to buy fresh vegetables and fish. After breakfast, I walk to my new job. I work as a receptionist at a boutique hotel downtown. The guests are friendly, and my manager is very kind. My sister Thida lives five minutes away from me. She works at a bank, and she often visits me after work. We cook dinner together and watch Korean dramas on television. Do you remember our holiday last August? We were in Ngapali Beach for five wonderful days. The weather was sunny and the sea was warm and blue. I miss those relaxing days together! Would you like to come and stay at my flat next weekend? We can visit the Shwedagon Pagoda and drink sweet milk tea at my favourite teashop. You should come in November when the weather is cooler and pleasant. Write back soon! Warm wishes, Su Su
